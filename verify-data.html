Verify Your Data

Upload a verification_data CSV to check it for validity before it's submitted — the same checks as our verifyData.py script, run entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server except a country-name lookup sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service.

Cross-File Checks

How this works: your file is parsed and checked entirely on your device — energy sums, timestamps, and formatting are all validated locally. To run cross-file checks (overlap detection, matching project metadata, and contracts.json coverage), this page also fetches the other CSVs listed in verification_data/files.json and web/js/contracts.json from this site, and sends the "Country" field to Nominatim to confirm your coordinates fall within that country. No file content is sent anywhere.
